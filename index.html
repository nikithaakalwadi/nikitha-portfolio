How I Work

My Design Process

🔍
Step 01
Discover & Research

Contextual inquiry, surveys, stakeholder interviews, and field visits. I dig deep before touching a single wireframe.

🗺️
Step 02
Define & Synthesise

Empathy maps, affinity diagrams, journey maps. Turning raw data into sharp problem statements and design opportunities.

✏️
Step 03
Ideate & Prototype

From sketches to Figma flows. I prototype at the fidelity the question needs — not more, not less.

🧪
Step 04
Test & Iterate

Usability testing, heuristic evaluation, and feedback loops. Good design earns its way through evidence.

The Human Behind the Screens

About Me

I'm from Hubli, Karnataka — currently in Pune studying UX design at MIT Institute of Design. I've always been the person who notices things: why one app feels smooth and another annoying, why we click without thinking, or why we hesitate before pressing a button.

I like understanding those small, almost invisible moments. It's fascinating how much thought goes into something that's supposed to feel effortless.

Outside design, I run on coffee and comfort food (dosa will always win). I'm a full-time foodie, a part-time movie critic, happiest with friends or family. I love long drives — windows down, music loud, no real destination.

I do acrylic painting when I want to slow down, make crafts just because I feel like it, and edit reels that turn everyday moments into something share-worthy.

I design the same way I live — curious, intentional, and not afraid to try something new.
